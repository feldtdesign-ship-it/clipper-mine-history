# Rights

One row per file. The repo is public. Only material cleared for public use is in it. Anything not cleared is held off the repo and
listed at the bottom.

Status words: **Cleared** (fine for a public repo, with the credit shown), **Check** (likely fine, still to
confirm), **Private only** (may stay in the private repo, not public), **Not cleared** (needs permission).

Abbreviations: DTIC (Defense Technical Information Center), DGGS (Alaska Division of Geological and Geophysical
Surveys), OFR (Open-File Report), USGS (US Geological Survey), USMS (US Mineral Survey), FNSB (Fairbanks North
Star Borough), UAF (University of Alaska Fairbanks).
