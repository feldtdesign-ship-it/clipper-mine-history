# Organizations named in the record

Every organization below is named in a source in this repo. Each row says where.
Logos are not included yet. Logos are trademarks, and showing them can suggest that a company endorses this
project. Before any logo goes on the public site, decide how it will be used and check each owner's terms.
See RIGHTS.md.

Abbreviations: DTIC (Defense Technical Information Center), DGGS (Alaska Division of Geological and Geophysical
Surveys), OFR (Open-File Report), USGS (US Geological Survey), UAF (University of Alaska Fairbanks).

## The station, 1970 to 1972

| Organization | What it did, as the source says | Source and page |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Columbia University, Lamont-Doherty Geological Observatory | Contractor. Designed, installed and ran the station. | Lamont 1971 (DTIC AD0729918), cover (PDF p. 1) and program page (PDF p. 5) |
| ARPA (Advanced Research Projects Agency) | Sponsor. ARPA Order No. 1513 (1970 contract) and No. 1774 (1971 to 72 contract). | AD0729918 PDF p. 1 and p. 5; Lamont 1972 (DTIC AD0744130) PDF p. 5 |
| AFOSR (Air Force Office of Scientific Research) | Named as the "sponsoring military activity" on the report form. | AD0729918 report form, PDF p. 3 |
| Geotech | Maker of the three 30-second seismometers. | AD0729918 abstract, PDF p. 3 |
| Kinemetrics | Maker of the 100-second galvanometer. | AD0729918 abstract, PDF p. 3 |
| Specific Products | Maker of the time-signal radio receiver installed in the tunnel. | AD0729918 report p. 24 (PDF p. 31) |
| National Ocean Survey, College Observatory | Gave logistic support during the installation; possible time signal source. | AD0729918 report p. 24 (PDF p. 31) and Acknowledgments, report p. 42 (PDF p. 49) |
| University of Alaska, Geophysical Institute | Possible time signal source by telephone line. | AD0729918 report p. 24 (PDF p. 31) |
| NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), Albuquerque | Received all the station equipment at closeout in April 1972; later copied the tapes. | Lamont 1973 (DTIC AD0760585) report p. 5 (PDF p. 11) |
| Texas Instruments | Received the original magnetic tapes from the program after quality control. | AD0760585 report p. 5 (PDF p. 11) |
| VELA Seismological Center | Received the seismograms before they were filmed. | AD0760585 report p. 5 (PDF p. 11) |
