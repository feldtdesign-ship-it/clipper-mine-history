Story

Development work at the Clipper began in 1927, and the early workings were mined by hand. 1927: OFR 157 p. 1 (Document). Hand mining: D. Wietchy, 2000, in ARDF FB047 p. 101 (Reported).

In 1970 Columbia University's Lamont-Doherty station, paid for by ARPA (Advanced Research Projects Agency) through the Air Force Office of Scientific Research (AFOSR) for nuclear-test-detection research, moved into a side tunnel 130 m from the entrance, under 20 m of rock. Lamont 1971, report pp. 1, 11
